Kurz gesagt

Ab Version 1.3 zeigt Gloomgems Werbung von Google AdMob: freiwillige Videos gegen Belohnung und, selten, eine Anzeige zwischen zwei Levels. Alle Anzeigen sind nicht personalisiert, die App fragt nicht nach App-Tracking und liest unter iOS die Werbe-ID (IDFA) nicht aus; Google verarbeitet dabei Daten wie deine IP-Adresse (siehe „Werbung (Google AdMob)“). Darüber hinaus betreiben wir kein Tracking und geben deine Daten nicht an Dritte weiter; nur unsere Auftragsverarbeiter (siehe „Wo“) verarbeiten sie in unserem Auftrag. Ein Konto brauchst du nicht. Die App nutzt Online-Dienste mit einer zufälligen Spieler-ID: für das Postfach mit Nachrichten und Geschenken, für den Support, für die Prüfung von Käufen und zum Schutz vor Missbrauch. Dazu kommt eine anonyme Levelstatistik ohne jede Kennung, die du in der App jederzeit abschalten kannst. Deine Spieler-ID bleibt; Auskunft, Löschung und Widerspruch laufen per E-Mail mit deinem Spielercode (siehe „Auskunft, Löschung und Widerspruch“). Die Zahlung bei Käufen im Spiel wickeln Apple bzw. Google ab; Zahlungsdaten erhalten wir nicht.

Ohne iCloud-Anmeldung, oder wenn du iCloud für Gloomgems ausschaltest (Einstellungen › dein Name › iCloud), bleibt der Spielstand nur auf dem Gerät; löschst du dann die App, ist er weg, und eine Neuinstallation bekommt eine neue Spieler-ID. Sperren (ab Version 1.2.1)

Bei Missbrauch oder Manipulation können wir eine Spieler-ID sperren. Es gibt zwei Stufen. Bei einer vollständigen Sperre zeigt das Spiel statt Start und Karte einen Hinweis; die Sperre kann befristet oder unbefristet sein. Beim Ausschluss aus Wettbewerben spielst du weiter, aber die App meldet deine Sterne nicht an die Game-Center-Bestenliste (nur iPhone und iPad), startet kein neues Sternenrennen, und deine Versuche zählen für kein Event. Der Hinweis im Spiel nennt den Grund und deinen Spielercode und verweist auf den Support; bei einer vollständigen Sperre zeigt er auch die Dauer.

Hältst du eine Sperre für einen Irrtum, schreib uns eine E-Mail mit deinem Spielercode. Wir prüfen den Fall und heben die Sperre auf, wenn sie unberechtigt war. Unser Server markiert Werte, die nicht zusammenpassen, automatisch zur Prüfung; eine Markierung allein hat für dich keine Folgen. Über jede Sperre entscheidet ein Mensch; es gibt keine automatisierte Entscheidung im Sinne von Art. 22 DSGVO. Eine Sperre, die deine App schon kennt, gilt auf dem Gerät auch dann weiter, wenn die Online-Dienste nach einer Löschung durch den Support ruhen. This lets us notice when a receipt is passed on or copied; there is then no second credit and the case is marked for review. We delete this information three years after the last check. If you have your data deleted, the link to your player ID is removed; the checksum remains so the same receipt cannot be redeemed again. If the server cannot be reached, the check on your device alone decides. If the online services are switched off after a deletion by support, the app sends no receipt to us. The app passes the receipt on to no third party other than Apple or Google. Purchase handling uses a software package from Unity; we have switched off its transmission of purchase data to Unity, and the app never starts Unity online services.

The app remembers your purchases in your progress on the device: product, transaction number and time, as a gold or booster entry. This avoids crediting anything twice and lets the starter pack be found again after reinstalling. On iPhone and iPad this entry is part of the copy in your iCloud storage (see above); we have no access to it. With “Restore purchases” in the settings the app asks the store for your earlier purchases; the starter pack is credited again this way if it is missing. Gold cannot be restored. The legal basis is the purchase contract you conclude with Apple or Google and your request to the app (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for the check on our server, our legitimate interest in protection against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Report a problem

Up to version 1.2 a report is sent as an email from your mail app. From version 1.2.1 it goes to our server as described below.

With “Report a problem” in the settings or in the level menu you can tell us about a bug. Only what the form shows before sending is sent: the category and your text, app version and build, platform, device model, operating system and language, the app's most recent error messages and – if you report from a level – the level number and the state of the board (moves, goals, arrangement of the gems). A screenshot is attached only if you leave it ticked; it shows the screen just before the menu opened. Nothing is transmitted before you send.

With the online services (the normal case, iOS and Android) the report goes to our server, linked to your player ID so that we can reply in the game's mailbox; we remove the image's metadata. If you have no connection, the app keeps the report on your device (at most 5 reports, at most 7 days) and sends it as soon as possible. We delete reports after 180 days, screenshots after 90 days, and together with your other data if you ask for deletion.

Without the online services (for example after a deletion by support, until you turn them back on) the app instead opens a prepared email to our support (support@gloomgems.de). On iPhone and iPad it contains the screenshot if you leave it ticked; on Android it opens your email app with the prepared message, without a screenshot. You see the email before sending and send it yourself. It arrives on a mail server operated by us; we use it only to handle your request and delete it after 180 days.

The legal basis is handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, for a report via the server, our legitimate interest in a bug-free app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Error reports (from version 1.2.1)

If a program error occurs in the app, it sends us a short report: the type of error with a short error message, a shortened call stack without file paths, app version, platform, the screen on which the error occurred and the level number. Like all other requests, this one is signed so that only genuine apps can send reports; the report itself, however, is stored without a player ID and cannot be linked to you. We delete error reports after 90 days. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a stable app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). After a deletion by support the app sends nothing more until you turn the online services back on.

Anonymous level statistics (from version 1.2.1)

To find levels that are too hard or too easy, the app reports how the levels are played. These reports contain no identifier and cannot be linked to you or your device. The statistics are on from the start; you can turn them off at any time in the app's settings under “Anonymous level stats”.

What: for each level, the start and the result: level number, win, loss or quit, moves left, the number of tools and continues used, the stars for a win, and which attempt it was (at most “10 or more”). In addition, the rough length of a play session in five steps (for example “3 to 10 minutes”), plus app version, platform (iOS or Android) and game language.

What not: no player ID, no installation or device identifier, no advertising ID, no name, no location, no time of day. For each report our server only notes the day it arrived. We cannot connect two reports from the same player, and there is no connection to the player ID of the online services.

Why: to adjust levels (for example the number of moves) and to understand how long people play on average. We do not use the figures for advertising and do not pass them on.

Legal basis: the stored figures are not personal data. To the extent your IP address is technically processed during transmission, we rely on our legitimate interest in a fairly balanced game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The app does not store or read any identifier on your device for the statistics.

Where: the reports are sent encrypted (HTTPS) to the same server in Germany, operated by us, as the online services, but as a separate request without a player ID. During transmission the server technically sees your IP address; we store it neither in our data nor in access logs.

How long: individual reports are deleted after 90 days. Before that we combine them into totals per level and day (for example starts, wins, quits, moves left); we keep those totals for 2 years. For the support live view, each end of a level briefly creates an entry with level, result, platform and arrival time, without any link to a person; it is deleted after 60 minutes and is not in the backups.

Turning it off: if you turn the statistics off, the app sends nothing further and discards what had not been sent yet. We cannot look up or delete reports already sent, because they are not linked to anyone.

Earlier test versions: test versions up to November 2026 asked for consent and used a random installation identifier. We have deleted that data completely; the app removes the old identifier from your device the next time it starts.
